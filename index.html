💪 Body & Mind

A fitness & mental toughness curriculum, not an afterthought

Table tennis is decided in split seconds — by legs that haven't tired, a core that holds the stroke together, and a mind that stays calm at 9-all. So alongside stroke work, every training track includes a structured physical and mental programme, tracked with the same rigour as technique:
  • Dynamic warm-ups & agility-ladder footwork drills every session
  • Core & shoulder strength circuits built for racket-sport durability
  • Reaction-speed & hand-eye coordination drills
  • Match-simulation pressure drills to build composure under score pressure
  • Breathing & focus routines before matches
  • Basic yoga & guided meditation sessions for recovery and mental calm
  • Monthly fitness benchmarking alongside the technical assessment
Our Training Edge

Train Against Every Style. Be Ready for Every Opponent.

More Than a Table Tennis Academy — We Train You for the Real Match.

Most players train almost exclusively against conventional inverted rubbers. But tournament table tennis is bigger than that, so our coaching goes beyond it too.

🏓 Our Multi-Rubber Training System. In competition you'll face short pips, long pips, anti-spin, defensive choppers, blockers, flat hitters and heavy topspin attackers — and each one produces a completely different ball. Our academy maintains dedicated training rackets across different rubber technologies, so players can experience, understand, and learn to defeat every style before they ever meet it in a match.
Rubber TypeWhat Students Learn
Inverted RubberModern topspin, spin variation, serve & receive, looping and counter-looping
Short PipsHandling fast, flat, low-spin attacks, blocks and quick counters
Long PipsReading unusual spin, trajectory changes, chops, blocks and spin reversal
Anti-Spin / Super AntiPlaying against dead balls, reduced-spin returns and deceptive changes
Gorilla / Anti-TopspinDeveloping solutions against disruptive blocking and highly unusual returns
Defensive RubbersBuilding patience, opening against defence and constructing points
Tacky / High-Spin InvertedLearning to deal with heavy rotation, loaded serves and high-spin loops

Short pips are especially useful for understanding fast hitting, blocking and counter-attacking — while long pips and anti-spin introduce fundamentally different spin-response characteristics.

🎯 Why This Gives Our Players An Edge

We don't just teach you how to play your game — we teach you how to solve your opponent's.

  • Recognise different rubber characteristics
  • Read unfamiliar spin and ball behaviour
  • Adjust technique instead of relying on one stroke
  • Attack defensive and disruptive players with confidence
  • Develop better serve & receive decisions
  • Understand what the opponent is trying to create
  • Get comfortable against styles they rarely see in regular practice
Tournament-ready, not tournament-surprised. A player who has only trained against inverted rubber can be thrown off by an opponent using long pips or anti-spin. Our players don't discover those rubbers for the first time in a tournament — they've already trained against them, already experienced the ball, and already worked out the solution.
Train against more styles. Understand more balls.
Develop more solutions. Play with more confidence.
