Our Vision

Development you can measure

We build table tennis ability the way a coach builds a rally — point by point, structured, and repeatable. Every player follows a defined pathway from Foundation to Elite, with a monthly assessment and an Individual Development Plan tracking footwork, stroke consistency, and match temperament — guided by our NIS-certified head coach and reinforced with robotic ball-feed training for precise, data-backed practice.

Structured curriculum

Every tier has a defined skill checklist — grip, footwork, spin, tactics — so progress is a checklist, not a guess.

NIS-certified coaching

Sessions led by our NIS-certified head coach, with a coaching-to-player ratio that stays low as players move up a tier.

Robot-assisted drilling

Precision ball-feed training builds repeatable strokes at controlled speed and spin — practice that's consistent every time.

Why Pratichi

What most academies get wrong

We built the program around the five things parents tell us they wish coaching did better.

Pain point — Lack of individual attention

An Individual Development Plan for every student

Instead of one coach running a large batch through identical drills, each player gets a development plan built around their own strengths and weaknesses, guided by our NIS-certified coach.

"Not every child gets the same training."
Pain point — Progress you can't see

A monthly assessment and progress report

Parents often pay fees for months without knowing what's actually improved. We report on technique, consistency, footwork, serves, and match skills every month.

"See your child's progress, not just hear about it."
Pain point — Coaching that plateaus

A complete player development pathway

Many students learn strokes, play matches, and stop there. Ours is a defined pathway: Assessment → Coaching → Robot Practice → Match Practice → Tournament → Review → Next Training Plan.

"A clear pathway from learning to competition."
Pain point — Not enough quality repetition

Robot-assisted structured repetition

A human coach can't feed the same shot at the same pace hundreds of times. Our training robot can — freeing the coach to focus on correction and tactics instead of just feeding balls.

"More quality repetitions. Better use of coaching time."
Pain point — Location & convenience

A high-quality neighbourhood academy

Long travel times and hard-to-find slots keep families away. We serve Alkapur, Manikonda, and Narsingi with convenient morning and evening batches on a clearly defined schedule.

"High-quality coaching close to home."
Coaching & Facilities

Who and what trains you

Head Coach

NIS-Certified Head Coach

Our head coach holds NIS certification and has trained players across every level, from first-time beginners to district and state-level competitors. Coaching focuses on technique correction, match temperament, and tournament readiness — with individual attention that increases as players move up the pathway.

Training Technology

Table Tennis Robot

Our ball-feed robot delivers controlled spin, speed, and placement so players can drill a single stroke hundreds of times with consistent input — something a human feeder can't match. Advanced and Elite players get dedicated robot sessions built into their weekly schedule.

Pro Shop & Racket Lab

Gear that grows with your game

We sell table tennis rackets, blades, and rubbers — and we build and service them in-house: re-pasting, regripping, edge taping, and full racket health checks. New players start on one simple, reliable racket; every upgrade after that is a coach recommendation, not a guess.
